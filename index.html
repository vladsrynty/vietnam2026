Essential Travel Tips

  • Visas & Entry: Check Vietnam e-visa requirements well ahead of December 2026. Ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity.
  • Domestic Flights: Keep an eye on luggage weight restrictions for regional airlines (e.g., DLI to DAD and DAD to VCA).
  • Weather in December: Central Vietnam (Đà Nẵng, Hội An) can experience occasional rain or cooler breezes—keep flexible days for surf/tunnels as planned! South and Highlands are generally pleasant.
  • Transport: Pre-book private transfers for Cát Tiên and Đà Lạt routes for a smooth trip.
